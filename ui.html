Search index

Confirmed meetings are stored in Postgres (pgvector) as summary points, decisions, action items and one-minute transcript passages, each with its labels, for search by meaning (and later the MCP server). Meetings are added when you confirm their labels and kept in step when you move, rename or delete them.

MCP access

Every tool call that Claude, Codex or another app made to the MCP server (http://127.0.0.1:8765/mcp), newest first: which app, which tool, which meetings it was given and how much. The text itself isn't logged. “Notes” means summaries, decisions and action items; “transcript” means transcript text. The app may send what it got to its own model provider. Log file: logs/mcp/mcp-audit.jsonl.

Summarise the queue automatically

Anything in “To summarise” is summarised on its own, oldest first, one at a time.
